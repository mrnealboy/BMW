51 16 ... Removing and installing/replacing protective cap on mirror base







Necessary preliminary work:
- Remove exterior mirror on front door







Pull the protective cap (1) off the mirror base (2) toward the inside.

Installation note:
Fit protective cap (1) flush with mirror base edge.
