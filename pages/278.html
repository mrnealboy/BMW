00 00 612 Service - Front Brakes

- Service - front brakes
- Change brake pads , clean brake air ducts
- Brake discs
- Check surface and thickness
- If necessary: Replace

- Reset CBS display according to factory specification

NOTE:
- Clean brake air ducts.
- Replace brake discs if they are below the minimum brake disc thickness and if scoring, heat cracks or thickness difference are detected.
- Apply a thin coat of grease to wheel centering on light alloy wheels, refer to Service Information Operating Fluids.
- Issue a separate invoice for replacing the brake discs.
