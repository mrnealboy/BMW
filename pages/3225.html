52 26 200 Removing and installing/replacing rear panel on right rear seat back

Special tools required:







Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove rear seat back







Lever backrest cover welt all round out of backrest frame using special tool 00 9 317.







Detach rear panel (2) with flat scraper (1) from rear seat back.

Installation:
Comply with instructions issued by manufacturer of universal spray adhesive.
Apply universal spray adhesive to rear panel and rear seat back.
Fit and align rear panel within the air drying time (see manufacturer's instructions).
Observe and adhere to curing time to prevent rear panels from slipping.
