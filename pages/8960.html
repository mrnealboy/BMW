Air Filter Element: Service and Repair



13 72 001 Replacing Air Filter Element (N52/ N52K/ N53)







Unlock air intake hose (1) sideways and detach.
Release screws (2) along line.
Remove top section (2) of intake filter housing.







Release locks (1) in direction of arrow and slide air filter element (2) in direction of arrow.
Remove air filter element (2).

Installation:
Clean upper and lower sections of intake filter housing from inside.
Install new air filter element.







Reassemble the vehicle.
