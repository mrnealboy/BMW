Removing and Installing (Replacing) Armrest on Door Trim Panel, Front Left or Right




51 41 060 - Removing and installing (replacing) armrest on door trim panel, front left or right





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove front door handle Removing and Installing or Replacing Door Handle on Door Trim Panel, Front Left





Important!
Risk of damage!
Only drill down to door trim panel area (2) so as not to damage the contact surface.

Drill out welding spots (1) on door trim panel (2) with 8 mm dia. welding spot drill bit.

Remove welding residue from door trim panel (2) and armrest with a scalpel (for heavy-duty applications) if these components are to be reused.





Replacement:
Saw off welding pins (1) on armrest (2) down to measurement (A).
A= - 2.0 ± 0.5 mm





Installation:
Armrest:
- position on door trim panel (1)
- secure at front and rear with repair screw (2)
- align until bores (3) are centrally located
- tighten down, starting in middle

Tightening torque 51 41 2AZ Specifications.

Note:
If bores (3) have not been correctly drilled through, increase tightening torque according in order to ensure that armrest is firmly seated.
