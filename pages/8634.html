36 11 121 - Removing and installing/replacing an RDC trigger transmitter (front)





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Partially detach front wheel arch trim 51 71 038 Removing and Installing/Replacing Front Left or Right Wheel Arch Cover (Front Section)





Press through studs (1) of expander rivets towards rear.
Remove trigger transmitter (2).

Installation:
If necessary, replace expander rivets.

- Carry out resetting of RDC
