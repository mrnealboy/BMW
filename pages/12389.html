Rear Bumper Bracket: Service and Repair




51 12 801 - Replacing bracket for rear bumper at left or right





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove rear bumper trim





If necessary, feed aerial cable (1) out of holder.

Installation:
Ensure correct lead routing.





Release screws (1).
Release nuts (2) and remove holder (3).





Installation:
Catches (1) on holder (2) must not be damaged.
