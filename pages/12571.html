51 16 195 - Removing and installing center armrest





Special tools required:
- 00 9 317





Lever out cover (1) and release nut (2) underneath.





Lever out catches (1) on trim (2) with special tool 00 9 317 and remove.





Installation:
Catches (1) on trim (2) must not be damaged.





Release screws (1) and nuts (2) on center armrest (3).





Raise storage compartment (1) slightly and extend center armrest (2) in downwards direction.
