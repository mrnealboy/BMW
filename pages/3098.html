FZD Roof Functions Centre



FZD Roof Functions Centre


NOTE:
- If the roof function centre (FZD) is fitted, it controls the following functions:
- Interior lights at front and rear
- Reading lights at front and rear
- Ambient light at front and rear
- slide/tilt sunroof (SHD)

For vehicles with a panorama glass roof, this is controlled by the extra sliding/tilting sunroof.

Initialization of slide/tilt sunroof (SHD)
The slide/tilt sunroof is initialized as follows:
- Press the slide/tilt sunroof switch for at least 15 seconds to switch position Lift and hold it down.
- Only let go of the slide/tilt sunroof switch after the SHD has moved to all positions and has come to a standstill.
