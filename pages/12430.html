Front Door Weatherstrip: Service and Repair




51 71 000 - Removing and installing / replacing seal on front door, left or right





Note:
Work shown on E92 way of example, deviations in detail are possible in other vehicle models.





Unclip seal (1) in direction of arrow from front door.

Installation:
Make sure seal (1) is correctly seated on window guide (2).





Installation:
Clips (1) on seal (2) must not be damaged.
