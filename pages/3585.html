Signal Names With P



Signal Names With P

PA Signal, gearbox-position switch
P_CLKS1 Clock pulse, guide and reference sensors VVT
P_CS1S1 Enable, guide sensor VVT
P_CS2S Signal, eccentric-shaft sensor
P_EVZ1 Fuel injector, cylinder 1
P_EVZ2 Fuel injector, cylinder 2
P_EVZ3 Fuel injector, cylinder 3
P_EVZ4 Fuel injector, cylinder 4
P_KWG Signal, crankshaft sensor
P_LOCK Signal, interlock sensor, selector lever
P/N Signal, selector lever position Park/Neutral
P_NWGA1 Exhaust-camshaft sensor 1
P_NWGE1 Intake-camshaft sensor 1
POL lamp, passenger-airbag deactivation
POS+ lamp, passenger-airbag deactivation+
POS- lamp, passenger-airbag deactivation-
POS_D Switch center, position D
POS_N Switch center, position N
POS_P Switch center, position P
POS_R Switch center, position R
P_SUP 0
PT_CAN_H Power train, CAN High bus signal
PT_CAN_L Power train, CAN Low bus signal
PTC_BF Heated washer nozzle, front passenger's side
PTC_FA Heated washer nozzle, driver's side
PWM_AUC Signal pulse-width-modulated, AUC sensor
PWM_GEBL Signal pulse-width-modulated, fan
P_ZSZ1 Ignition coil, cylinder 1
P_ZSZ2 Ignition coil, cylinder 2
P_ZSZ3 Ignition coil, cylinder 3
P_ZSZ4 Ignition coil, cylinder 4
