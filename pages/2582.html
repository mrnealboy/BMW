Fault Symptom Overview: ULF-SBXMOST



Fault Symptom Overview: ULF-SBXMOST

Perceived symptoms are available for the following problem groups:
- Telephone operation (insufficient voice quality, poor reception quality, ...)
- Bluetooth pairing and connection
- Telephone functions (phone book, contacts, SMS text message, expanded functions, ...)
- Charging the mobile phone, snap-in adapter
- Repair, programming, maintenance, pre-delivery check

General notes on the use of a mobile phone in the vehicle
- The compatibility matrix and more detailed fault descriptions for the mobile phone can be found on the Internet at the following link: www.bmw.com/bluetooth or www.mini.com/connectivity
- No more than one minute must elapse between switching on the mobile phone and PIN input in the mobile phone.
- If possible, enter the PIN in the mobile phone before starting the vehicle.
- With complex problems not shown in the compatibility matrix, switch off the mobile phone. If possible, remove the battery, wait for a short time, then switch it back on and reconnect it to the vehicle. Cause: Mobile phone software can crash or hang up.
