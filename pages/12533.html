51 14 010 - Removing and installing/replacing rear BMW badge





Special tools required:





Important!
The Instructions on component cementing with double-sided adhesive tape serve as the basis for this repair instruction and must be observed without fail.





Heat BMW badge (1) with hot air blower and lever out with special tool 00 9 322.





Installation Note:
Pull liner* off adhesive tape (1).

Position badge over guides (2 and 3) and press down firmly.

* Liner is the protective film on a new badge.
