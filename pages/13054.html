Driver/Vehicle Information Display: Description and Operation



CID Folding Mechanism For CID, Central Information Display


CID folding mechanism:
The Central Information Display (CID) is the indicating instrument of the headunit. The CID has a folding mechanism that is opened on the terminal change from "terminal R off" to "terminal R on". On terminal change to "terminal R off", the CID closes.
The CID can be closed in the corresponding menu item using the controller. The command for closing is sent by the headunit via the K-CAN to the CID. A closed CID is opened again by pressing a button on the controller. The command for opening is also sent by the headunit via the K-CAN to the CID.
The angle of inclination of the CID can be set by hand.
E87 only: When it is closed, the CID saves the last position. The next time it is folded open, the last position saved by the CID is assumed.
Only E89: The CID always opens completely, even if the angle was manually adjusted beforehand.
