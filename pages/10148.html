51 16 210 Removing and Installing/Replacing Trim For Preselector Lever




51 16 210 - Removing and installing/replacing trim for preselector lever





Important!
Risk of damage!
When working on trim panel components, make sure that the sensitive surfaces are not scratched or damaged.





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Apply parking brake
- Remove gaiter for shift tower 25 16 065 Replacing Gaiter (Selector Lever Cover) For Shift Tower





Unclip trim for preselector lever (1) in upwards direction from trim (2).
Disconnect associated plug connections and remove trim for preselector lever (1) towards top.





Installation Note:
Catches (1) on preselector lever trim (2) must not be damaged.
