51 12 825 - Removing and installing/replacing guide for rear centre bumper





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove rear bumper trim
Version with Comfort Access/CA:
- Remove bumper aerial for Comfort Access [1][2]61 35 975 Removing and Installing/Replacing Bumper Antenna For Comfort Access System





If necessary, feed out cable (1) of Park Distance Control/FM aerial.
Release nuts (2) and remove guide (3).

Installation:
Ensure correct lead routing.





Installation:
Catches (1) on guide (2) must not be damaged.
