54 12 138 - Removing and installing/replacing rain gutter for glass slide/tilt sunroof





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove glass slide/tilt sunroof lid Service and Repair





Note:
If activating the function when removed:
- It is essential to ensure that the slide of rain gutter (1) is situated ahead of connecting element (2).
- If the slide of rain gutter (1) is situated below the connecting element, this will result in a collision and damage.





Lever connecting element (2) on left and right out of rain gutter linkage (1).

Installation:
Linkage must be felt to snap into place in connecting element.





Unclip linkage of rain gutter (1) on left and right out of connecting element slide (2) towards rear.





Important!
Carry out function check only with rain gutter fitted (risk of damage).
