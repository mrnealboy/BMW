Removing and Installing/Replacing Lip on Underbody Panelling on Left or Right




51 47 ... - Removing and installing/replacing lip on underbody panelling on left or right





Note:
Prior to removing, pay attention to the exact installation position with respect to the adjoining components.
The operation is described on the left side; proceed in the same way for the right side.





Release expansion rivets (1).
Release catches (2).
Feed out lip (3) towards rear from underbody panelling and remove.

Installation:
If necessary, replace faulty expander rivets (1).





Installation:
Catches (1) on lip (2) must not be damaged.
