51 45 460 - Removing and installing/replacing upper instrument panel storage compartment





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove centre fresh air grille





Open storage compartment (1) and remove insert (2).





Release screws (1) and (2).
Raise storage compartment out of instrument panel until plug connection (3) can be disconnected. Disconnect plug connection and remove storage compartment from instrument panel.
