Trunk Light Bulb: Service and Repair




63 99 451 - Replacing light bulb for luggage compartment lighting





Warning!
Follow instructions for handling light bulbs (interior lights) Service and Repair.





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove luggage compartment light





Move lens cover (1) in direction of arrow and remove.
Remove festoon (2) underneath.

Installation Note:
Note type of bulb 63 31 Interior Lights.
