31 33 100 Removing And Installing/Replacing Coil Spring For Left Or Right Front Spring Strut







IMPORTANT:
Both coil springs on the relevant axle must be replaced only in the event of corrosion breakage.

NOTE: The coil spring is allocated in the Electronic Parts Catalogue (EPC) under the menu item "Spring table" after the vehicle identification number has been entered and the relevant optional equipment of the car has been selected.







NOTE: Procedure is described in the document "Replacing front left or right spring strut".
