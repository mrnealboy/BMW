Parking Light Bulb: Service and Repair




63 99 132 - Replacing bulb for front left (or right) side light (after 03/2007)





Warning!
Follow instructions for handling light bulbs (exterior lights) .





Note:
Headlight (2) removed for purposes of clarity.

Unlock spring wire clips (1) in direction of arrow.
Remove cover from headlight (2).

Installation Note:
Make sure cover is correctly seated on headlight (2).
Guides (3) must be exactly seated in associated locators (4).





Grip bulb holder (1) on both sides and pull towards rear out of headlight (2).

Installation Note:
Make sure bulb holder (1) is exactly seated in headlight (2).





Withdraw bulb for side marker lamp (1) in direction of arrow from bulb holder (2).

Installation Note:
Note type of bulb Specifications.
