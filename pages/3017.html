Removing and Installing/Replacing Housing on Left or Right Door Mirror




51 16 040 - Removing and installing/replacing housing on left or right door mirror





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove mirror glass [1][2]51 16 026 Replacing Mirror Glass





Important!
Risk of damage!
Secure housing (2) against falling off.

Note:
Housing (2) is secured without play by four multitooth catches (1) on retaining ring (3).

Unlock multitooth catches (1) with a screwdriver.
Unclip housing (2) forwards from retaining ring (3) and remove.





Installation:
Multitooth catches (1) and guide (2) on housing (3) must not be damaged.





Installation:
Locators (1) on retaining plate (2) must not be damaged.
