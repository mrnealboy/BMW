33 11 271 - Replacing rear cover gasket on rear differential





Important!
In event of oil loss, always check rear differential for traces of wear and damage.





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Draw off/drain differential oil 00 11 259 Oil Change In Rear Differential Incl. Used Oil Disposal
- Remove rear differential 33 10 010 Removing And Installing/Replacing Rear Differential





Note:
Read and comply with document "Rear differential: Assignment to model series Application and ID".

Select rear differential:
- 168L
- 188L / 188LW 33 11 ... Replacing Rear Cover Gasket on Rear Differential (188L/188LW)
- 215L / 215LW 33 10 010 Removing And Installing/Replacing Rear Differential
- 215 with lock





After installation:
- Add rear differential oil 00 11 259 Oil Change In Rear Differential Incl. Used Oil Disposal
