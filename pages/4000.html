00 00 612 Preliminary Check Prior To Road Worthiness Test

- Preliminary check prior to roadworthiness test:
- Check headlight adjustment
- Visually inspect steering system
- Visually inspect axles, suspension, springs, anti-roll bar, vibration damper, axle damping, if necessary air suspension
- Visually inspect silencers
