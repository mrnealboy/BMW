License Plate Lamp: Service and Repair



63 26 000 Removing And Installing/replacing Left Or Right Number/license Plate Light

Special tools required:







IMPORTANT: Follow instructions for handling light bulbs (exterior lights).







Lever out licence plate light (1) with special tool 64 1 020 in direction of arrow (A) from tailgate (2).
Remove number/license plate light (1) in direction of arrow (B).
Disconnect plug connection underneath.

Replacement:
If necessary, remove bulb.
Note bulb type.
