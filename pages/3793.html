AHL Adaptive Headlights



AHL Adaptive Headlights

WARNING: When you are working on the bi-xenon headlights, observe the accident prevention regulations. The headlight system has dangerous high voltage.

To switch on the Adaptive Headlight:
- Switch on terminal 15
- Turn the light switch to position "A"
- Darken the rain / driving light sensor

NOTE: Depending on the encoding, the Head Light moves only in one direction or not at all when stationary.
