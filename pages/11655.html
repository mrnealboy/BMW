65 50 055 Removing and Installing/Replacing Central Information Display



65 50 055 Removing And Installing/Replacing Central Information Display







IMPORTANT: Read and comply with notes on protection against electrostatic damage (ESD protection).







Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove centre fresh-air grille
- Remove Car Communication Computer







Open monitor (1) as illustrated.
Unfasten screws (2).







Release screws (1).
Disconnect plug connection (2) and release associated wiring harness.

Installation:
Make sure wiring harness is correctly laid and secured.

Raise central information display (3) on passenger side. Disconnect plug connection (4).
Feed out associated wiring harness and remove central information display (3).

Installation:
Make sure wiring harness of central information display (3) is correctly routed.
