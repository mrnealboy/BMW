Oil level OK: 3/4 above minimum. approx. 0.4 liters
Topping up oil not necessary.
Oil level OK: at maximum. Do
not top up oil

Prerequisites for oil level measurement:
- Oil at operating temperature (over 65 °C).
- Engine at idle or driving.

How the measurement works
The measurement functions according to the following principle:
- If less than 0.4 liters of oil is added, it is not immediately detected.
- If 0.4 to 0.8 liters of oil is added, the system detects that oil has been added after approx. 2 minutes.
If this quantity of added oil is detected, the display is as follows: Oil level OK, 3/4 above minimum

- If more than 0.8 liters of oil is added, the system detects that oil has been added after approx. 2 minutes.
If this quantity of added oil is detected, the display is as follows: Oil level OK, at maximum

- Only after a longer period (at least 10 minutes) with the engine running at over 1000 rpm and the engine oil at operating temperature is the exact oil level determined, e. g. Oil level OK, 3/4 above minimum

Example:
- The oil level is 2 liters short of maximum
- Measurement returns Oil level below minimum
- 1 litre is added as per instructions
- Repeat measurement returns Oil level OK, at maximum
- After the vehicle has been running for a longer period, the precise reading is shown Oil level OK, 1/2 above minimum

This is not an indication of a faulty sensor, exact measurement simply takes that long.
