Symptom Related Diagnostic Procedures



Fault Patterns BMW Professional Radio

On the BMW Professional radio (RAD2), fault patterns that are not registered by fault entries can occur alongside the possible fault entries. The following table lists the most frequent fault patterns. The appropriate measure for fault elimination is described in the testing procedure. Select the fault pattern from the list and enter the corresponding number.

General

No. Perceived symptom
001 In general, device without function
002 No switch-on via rotary push button
003 No automatic shutdown with ignition key removed
004 No automatic switch-on when switching on terminal 30 g
005 Radio disruption due to brake operation (only E9x)
006 Cannot be switched on at terminal 30 (only E9x with auxiliary heater)
