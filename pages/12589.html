51 46 ... - Removing and installing/replacing cover for rear left or right seat belt





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove trim panel on roof pillar at rear Removing and Installing/Replacing Trim Panel For Left or Right Rear Roof Pillar





Release clip (1).
Feed luggage compartment wheel arch trim (2) out of rest side section (3) and cover (4).

Note:
Feed out luggage compartment wheel arch trim (2) at top only to facilitate access to screw underneath.





Release clips (1) and screw (3).
Slide cover (2) in direction of arrow and feed out of rear parcel shelf (4).
Feed cover (2) out of belt and remove.
