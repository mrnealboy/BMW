65 77 740 - Removing and installing (replacing) left front door sensor





Important!
Read and comply with notes on protection against electrostatic damage (ESD protection) 61 35 ... Notes on ESD Protection (Electro Static Discharge).





Warning!
Note airbag safety instructions [1][2]Safety Regulations For Handling Components With Gas Generators!
Incorrect handling can activate airbag and cause injury.





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove door trim panel Removing and Installing (Replacing) Front Left or Right Door Trim Panel





Detach acoustic insulation (1) in working area (2).





Release screw (1).
Feed out sensor towards bottom and disconnect associated plug connection.
Tightening torque 65 77 7AZ 65 77 Airbag Triggering Control.

Installation Note:
Make sure sealing ring (3) is correctly seated on sensor (2).
