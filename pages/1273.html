23 00 038 - Installing replacement transmission (GS6-17BG/DG)





Important!
After completion of work, check transmission fluid level Service and Repair.
Use only the approved transmission fluid.

Failure to comply with this instruction will result in serious damage to the transmission.





Recycling:
Catch and dispose of escaping transmission fluid.
Observe country-specific waste-disposal regulations





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Drain gear oil at oil drain plug. Tightening torque:23 00 4AZ [1][2]23 00 Transmission in General 6 Speed

- Remove gearbox 23 00 017 Removing and Installing Transmission (GS6-17BG)





Convert shift rod joint.
Push back retaining ring (1) and drive out dowel pin (2).





Note:
Check damping disk (6) for damage and replace if necessary.





Convert following parts from previous transmission to new transmission.
- Release bearing and release lever Service and Repair

- Ball pin and spring wire clip

- Studs for clutch slave cylinder

- Reversing light switch, tightening torque: 23 11 1AZ [1][2]23 14 Electrical Fittings.





Add final details to vehicle.
Check oil level.

Installation Note:
Observe greasing specification 23 00 017 Removing and Installing Transmission (GS6-17BG).
