51 71 ... - Removing and installing/replacing right spring support passage cover





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- If necessary, remove right front wheel 36 10 300 Removing or Installing Front or Rear Wheel.





Note:
Description is shown on the E82 by way of example. There may be differences in detail in the case of other models.





Note:
Spring strut removed for purposes of clarity.

Release expander rivet (1) and remove cover (2).

Installation:
If necessary, replace expander rivet (1).
Note correct position of cover (2).
