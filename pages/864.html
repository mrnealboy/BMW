Nominal Values For Accelerator Pedal Module

Nominal voltage values of the accelerator pedal module signals in V:

Sensor 1 idle speed 0,70-0,80
Sensor 2 idle speed 0,31-0,43
Sensor 1 full load 3,65-4,10
Sensor 2 full load 1,83-2,04
Sensor 1 pressure point for 4,27-4,325
kickdown
Sensor 2 pressure point for 2,135-2,162
kickdown
Sensor 1 mechanical limit position less than 4.76
Sensor 2 mechanical limit position less than 2.38
