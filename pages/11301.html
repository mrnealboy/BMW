36 10 209 Checking front and rear wheel for lateral and radial runout

Special tools required:





Necessary preliminary tasks:
^ Check wheel bearings





Raise vehicle.

Check wheel with special tool 36 1 030 (measuring device) for lateral runout.





Check wheel with special tool 36 1 030 (measuring device) for radial runout.





If applicable, remove balance weights.

Check lateral runout of wheel rim with special tool 36 1 030 (measuring device).





Check radial runout of wheel rim with special tool 36 1 030(measuring device).
