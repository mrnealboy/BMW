Hood Sensor/Switch (For Alarm): Service and Repair



61 31 241 Removing And Installing/replacing Bonnet/hood Contact Switch







Disconnect plug connection (1).
Release catches (2) and feed bonnet/hood contact switch (3) in direction of arrow out of fixture (4).

Installation:
Make sure bonnet/hood contact switch (3) is correctly seated in holder (4).
