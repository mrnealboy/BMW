51 45 181 - Removing and installing/replacing bottom right trim for instrument panel





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- If necessary, remove footwell light 63 31 020 Removing and Installing/Replacing Footwell Light (Footwell).





Note:
Move front passenger seat completely towards rear.
Description is shown on the E87 by way of example. There may be differences in detail in the case of other models.





Release expansion rivet (1).
Release screws (2) on bottom right instrument panel trim (3).
Feed out bottom right instrument panel trim (3) towards bottom front.





Installation:
Attachments (1) on bottom right instrument panel trim (2) must not be damaged.
