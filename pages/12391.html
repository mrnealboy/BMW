51 12 ... Replacing Rear Bumper Trim




51 12 ... - Replacing rear bumper trim (M Technic aerodynamic kit)





Special tools required:





Important!
The Instructions on component cementing with double-sided adhesive tape serve as the basis for this repair instruction and must be observed without fail.





Remove trim:

Carefully detach trim (1) from rear bumper with special tool 00 9 317 and hot air blower.





Installation Note:
Pull protective film (1) off trim (2).

Fit trim (2) over guides (3) into associated mountings on bumper and stick on.
