65 77 580 - Removing and installing seat position sensor for left or right front seat





Warning!
Note airbag safety instructions!
Incorrect handling can activate airbag and cause injury.





Necessary preliminary tasks:
- Remove front seat Removing and Installing Left or Right Front Seat (Normal / Manual)





Cut through cable tie (1).
Release screw (2) and remove seat position sensor (3) from seat rail.

Installation Note:
Replace cable tie (1).
Ensure correct cable routing (4).
